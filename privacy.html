Privacy Policy

This policy describes Vuonglife Publisher, a local desktop application operated by Anthony Vuong. It also describes this static support website.

Information the application uses

When an account is connected, the application uses OAuth access and refresh tokens and account identifiers to verify the selected destination. For an approved upload it uses the video you select, its title or caption, visibility settings, and supported disclosure settings. Local plans and logs can contain file paths, file hashes, account identifiers, upload status, and resulting media links.

Storage and sharing

The application stores credentials in macOS Keychain. Non-secret connection settings, upload plans and operational records are stored on the operator’s Mac or selected local drive. Video files remain at their selected local locations unless an authorized upload or hosting step sends them elsewhere.

Authorized data is sent to Google/YouTube or TikTok to connect the account, perform the approved upload and retrieve its status. A separately authorized media host may receive a selected file when a destination requires a hosted URL. The application does not sell account information or use Google or TikTok account data for advertising or model training.

Website

This support website does not provide login, upload forms, analytics scripts or advertising. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP address, browser details and access logs to deliver and secure the site.

Retention and control

Local credentials and records remain until removed by the operator. You can revoke authorization through your Google or TikTok account settings and remove the application’s entries in macOS Keychain. You can delete local connection files, plans and logs on your Mac. Revoking the application does not delete videos already uploaded to a platform; manage those in YouTube or TikTok.

Google API data

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact and changes

For access, deletion or privacy questions, contact vuonglifestyle@gmail.com. Updates will be published here with a revised date. Do not send passwords, tokens or client secrets by email or chat.

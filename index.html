Vuonglife Publisher is Anthony Vuong’s local desktop workflow for preparing finished videos for connected social accounts.

How it works

  1. Select a finished video and prepare its title, caption, visibility and disclosure instructions.
  2. Review the destination account and approve the specific upload plan.
  3. For YouTube, upload privately by default. For TikTok, send an inbox upload and finish editing and posting inside TikTok.

YouTube and TikTok connections are currently being configured and tested. This page does not provide account login or accept video uploads. Public Direct Post availability is not promised.

Your account stays yours

Account authorization happens on the platform’s own consent screen. The local application stores credentials in macOS Keychain. No video is uploaded merely by connecting an account.
